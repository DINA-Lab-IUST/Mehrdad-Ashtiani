Mehrdad Ashtiani is an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Engineering at Iran University of Science and Technology. His academic path connects software engineering, distributed simulation, computational trust, cloud systems, automatic refactoring, and socio-computational research.

01 / Academic story

One discipline.
Several evolving questions.

All three of Dr. Ashtiani's academic degrees are in Software Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology: BSc in 2009, MSc in 2011, and PhD in 2014.

His early academic work moved from game-engine implementation to distributed cyber-attack simulation, then to a doctoral focus on computational trust. That progression continues in a broader research portfolio spanning adaptive cloud and serverless systems, software evolution and refactoring, context-aware computing, and socio-computational systems.

Alongside research, he has maintained a substantial teaching and supervision record and a professional trajectory that reaches beyond academia into cloud infrastructure, academic-industry liaison, enterprise software, and Iran's computer-game ecosystem.
